M3JAR PRODUCT INTENT

M3Jar is a collaborative group savings application for shared goals such as trips, weddings, honeymoons, and similar events.

Core product concept:

- An organizer creates a Jar.
- Organizer defines:
  - goal/event
  - total cost
  - event/trip date
  - number of participants
  - deadlines/milestones where applicable
- M3Jar calculates:
  - per-person contribution amount
  - weekly/monthly savings requirements
  - progress toward the group goal
- Organizer invites contributors by email.
- Invitee creates an account and joins the Jar.
- Each participant has their own contribution/progress tracking.
- Contributions must eventually represent REAL deposited funds, not simply “mark as saved.”
- Planned payments can be weekly/monthly and eventually support autopay.
- Funds can be withdrawn before the agreed non-refundable cutoff date.
- At cutoff, funds become non-refundable according to the group agreement.
- Organizer can eventually use the collected funds to pay for the trip/event.
- Users should receive transactional notifications/emails for important events.
- M3Jar plans to monetize through a fee when funds are withdrawn/used for the trip.
- Stripe/bank-link/payment infrastructure is intended for real-money functionality.
