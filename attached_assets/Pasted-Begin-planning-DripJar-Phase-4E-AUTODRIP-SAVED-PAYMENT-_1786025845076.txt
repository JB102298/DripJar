==================================================
PHASE 4E PRODUCT GOAL
==================================================

Add the ability for a contributor to:

1. Save a payment method securely through Stripe.
2. Choose an AutoDrip schedule.
3. Authorize DripJar to initiate future Drips automatically.
4. See:
   - AutoDrip amount
   - frequency
   - next scheduled Drip
   - saved payment method summary
   - projected completion date / progress
5. Pause or cancel AutoDrip.
6. Safely handle failed or delayed automatic payments.

Core user experience:

"Set it once. Drip toward it automatically."

3. AUTODRIP IS NOT A SUBSCRIPTION PRODUCT
==================================================

Do NOT model AutoDrip as a Stripe Subscription unless there is a compelling architectural reason.

DripJar AutoDrip is a Jar-specific savings schedule.

Example:

Vacation Jar
Contributor owes $2,000

AutoDrip:
$200 monthly

10 scheduled Drips

The amount may change if:

- member gets ahead
- member refunds money
- Jar target changes before launch where allowed
- schedule changes
- final remaining amount is smaller than the configured AutoDrip

Therefore prefer DripJar-controlled scheduling + PaymentIntents over a fixed Stripe subscription billing model.

Audit and confirm.
